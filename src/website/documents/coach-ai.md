### Step 02 — Medical Screening
Thirteen condition flags. Medications. Injuries. Allergies. Sleep quality. Cycle status for female users. Required, not optional. Each flag triggers real plan modifications — kidney disease caps protein at 0.6–0.8 g/kg, heart disease blocks HIIT until a clearance flag, pregnancy triggers an OB-GYN referral.

**Live screening card (13 flags):**

- ✓ Hypertension · negative
- ⚠ PCOS · flagged · anti-inflamm protocol
- ⚠ Lower back pain · no axial loading
- ✓ Kidney function · normal
- ✓ Allergies · none

Primary goal. Target date. Equipment on hand. Dietary preference. Regional cuisine. And then the question most apps never ask: **what foods do you actually have at home?** Your plan is built from that list, not an aspirational ingredient sheet you'll abandon by Friday.

## Section: FAQ
**Kicker:** FAQ
**Headline:** The questions *you'd ask* over coffee.

**Q: Is Coach AI a real trainer or a chatbot?**
It's a clinical engine using six expert role-systems — trainer, dietitian, sports medicine, clinical, behavioral, sport. It refuses to plan without screening, calculates everything openly, and refers serious cases to physicians. A chatbot generates. Coach AI assembles.

**Q: What if I have a medical condition?**
Tell it. Thirteen conditions are handled with automatic plan modifications. Kidney disease caps protein at 0.6–0.8 g/kg. Heart disease blocks HIIT until a clearance flag. Pregnancy triggers OB-GYN referral. Each rule is visible in the plan.

**Q: Does it know Indian and regional foods?**
Yes — Indian, South Indian, North Indian, Middle Eastern, East Asian, Latin, Western, Mixed. Plans are built from your real pantry, in your real cuisine, at your real budget tier.

**Q: Can it adapt my existing diet plan?**
Yes. Share your current plan — Coach AI reviews it for macro balance, calorie accuracy, and gaps, then refines rather than replaces. It respects the work you've already put in.

**Q: What about pregnancy or postpartum?**
Pregnancy triggers an OB-GYN referral — no training programmed without clearance. Postpartum under six months prioritizes pelvic floor and gradual reconditioning.

**Q: Does it work without internet?**
Yes. Plans, calculations, and history live on your device. Train, log, and review without a single bar of signal.

## FAQ

**Heading:** Questions, *answered.*

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Two taps, no phone call, no retention page. Your logs, PRs and plans stay in your account forever — downgrade or come back whenever.

**Q: What happens on the free plan?**
Free is genuinely free. You log workouts, track streaks, and get 3 coach messages a week. No trial timer. Most people start here and upgrade when the coach starts paying for itself.

**Q: Is yearly actually 30% off?**
Yes — ₹209/mo on Pro instead of ₹299. Billed once a year as ₹2,499. Swap to yearly anytime and we prorate the difference.

**Q: Who uses Team?**
Couples training together, families sharing meal plans, small gym squads, coaches running a 5-person program. Everyone gets their own Pro account — the payer just gets one bill and a shared board.

**Q: Do you store my health data?**
Encrypted end-to-end. We never sell it. You can export everything as CSV or delete your account (and all data) from settings in one tap.

**Q: Student or military discount?**
50% off Pro for students, teachers, active military and first responders. Verify with ID.me, get a code, apply it at checkout.

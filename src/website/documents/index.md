**Q: What's the catch?**
There isn't one. Optional expansion packs — sport-specific programs, regional cuisine libraries — exist as tiny one-time add-ons if you want them. The core app is yours outright.

**Q: Is the AI coach good enough to replace a human trainer?**
For most people training for strength, conditioning, or weight management — yes. For elite athletes, Lyftora works alongside your human coach as a 24/7 memory and logger. It's a tool, tuned carefully.

**Q: Does it work on older phones?**
Yes. 15 MB download, runs smoothly on 2 GB RAM, under 2 s cold start. We benchmarked on budget devices first — flagships came second.

**Q: When does iOS launch?**
End of June 2026, at the same price. Your purchase follows you — buy on Android, log in on iOS, no second charge.

**Q: What if I don't love it?**
Full refund inside 30 days, no forms, no guilt. We'd rather earn a fan for a decade than trap a user for a month.

01 · Foundations

Ink on paper. Sport, not tech-bro.

Lyftora's aesthetic is a warm cream surface, a near-black ink, and a short list of loud accents used with restraint. Serifs carry the voice; sans handles work; mono stamps everything with the confidence of a scoreboard.

01

Warm, not sterile.

Cream paper beats cold white. Ink is almost-black, never pure. Every surface should feel printed, not rendered.

02

Loud, but rare.

Lime, coral, violet, sky — the four accents punctuate, they don't decorate. Most surfaces live on cream or ink.

03

Serif for soul.

Instrument Serif italic is the brand's heartbeat — it carries every headline's emotional beat. Sans does the functional reading.

04

Pills everywhere.

Round shapes over rectangles. Buttons, tags, inputs, nav. Radius scales from 12 → 48 → 999. No sharp edges.

Do & Don't

Do
Use italic serif for emotional punctuation only.

One italic phrase per headline. Coral color makes it sing.

Don't
Don't italicize full sentences.

Serif italic is a spotlight, not a paragraph style.

Do
Pair lime with ink for trust + energy.

The brand's home chord. Use on CTAs, stickers, and highlights.

Don't
Don't place lime on cream without a border.

Contrast drops. Put it on ink, or give it an ink border.

Do
Use mono labels at 11–12px, uppercase, tracked.

Kickers, tags, stickers. Letter-spacing .1em minimum.

Don't
Don't use mono for body copy.

It's a stamp, not a voice. Keep it small and occasional.

07 · Voice & Motion

How it sounds & how it moves.

Voice

Short. Punchy. Earned.
Lyftora's voice is confident without swagger, warm without mushiness. Every sentence is a jab. Rhythm matters more than cleverness.
Use
Train smarter. Pay once. Lift forever.
Avoid
Synergy, ecosystem, unlock, journey, level up.

Motion principles

Weight Things have mass. Reveals rise 30px with .7s ease.
Bounce Reserved for wins — rep bumps, plate chips, PRs.
Continuous Tickers, stamps, blobs. Slow, hypnotic, paused on hover.
Reduced motion All animations disable under prefers-reduced-motion.
08 · Usage in code

Drop it into anything.

Paste the token block at the top of any stylesheet, and every component in this system renders correctly. Same tokens map 1:1 to React Native, Figma variables, iOS/Android theme files.

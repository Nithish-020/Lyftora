Yes. One plan unlocks the entire app — across every device you use. No locked tiers. No “premium” workouts. No upsell pop-ups mid-set.
What's the catch?
There isn't one. Optional expansion packs — sport-specific programs, regional cuisine libraries — exist as tiny one-time add-ons if you want them. The core subscription is everything you need.
Is the AI coach good enough to replace a human trainer?
For most people training for strength, conditioning, or weight management — yes. For elite athletes, Lyftora works alongside your human coach as a 24/7 memory and logger. It's a tool, tuned carefully.

Yes. 15 MB download, runs smoothly on 2 GB RAM, under 2 s cold start. We benchmarked on budget devices first — flagships came second.
